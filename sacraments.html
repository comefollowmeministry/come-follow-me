The Seven Sacraments

Visible signs instituted by Christ to give grace.

Baptism

Baptism is the first sacrament of Christian initiation. Through water and the Holy Spirit we become children of God, members of Christ's Body, and are freed from original sin.

Confirmation

Confirmation strengthens the grace of Baptism through the gift of the Holy Spirit, enabling Christians to witness faithfully to Christ.

The Holy Eucharist

The Eucharist is the source and summit of the Christian life. In Holy Communion, Catholics receive the true Body, Blood, Soul, and Divinity of Jesus Christ under the appearances of bread and wine.

Reconciliation (Confession)

Through Confession, Christ forgives sins committed after Baptism and restores the sinner to communion with God and the Church.

Anointing of the Sick

This sacrament gives spiritual strength, peace, courage, and, when God wills, physical healing to those who are seriously ill or elderly.

Holy Matrimony

Marriage is the lifelong covenant between one man and one woman, reflecting Christ's faithful love for His Church.

Holy Orders

Holy Orders consecrates bishops, priests, and deacons for service in the Church through preaching, sanctifying, and shepherding God's people.
