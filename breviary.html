About the Breviary

The Liturgy of the Hours is the official daily prayer of the Catholic Church. Through this prayer, the Church continues the praise of God offered by Christ.

Morning Prayer (Lauds)

Morning Prayer is offered at the beginning of the day, giving thanks to God for the gift of life and asking for His guidance.

Morning Prayer Includes:

  • Opening verse
  • Hymn
  • Psalmody
  • Scripture Reading
  • Responsory
  • Benedictus (Canticle of Zechariah)
  • Intercessions
  • Our Father
  • Closing Prayer

Evening Prayer (Vespers)

Evening Prayer gives thanks for the blessings of the day and places our lives into God's hands.

Evening Prayer Includes:

  • Opening verse
  • Hymn
  • Psalmody
  • Scripture Reading
  • Magnificat (Canticle of Mary)
  • Intercessions
  • Our Father
  • Closing Prayer

Night Prayer (Compline)

Night Prayer is the final prayer of the day, asking God for protection and peaceful rest.

Night Prayer Includes:

  • Examination of conscience
  • Psalm
  • Scripture Reading
  • Responsory
  • Nunc Dimittis
  • Final blessing
