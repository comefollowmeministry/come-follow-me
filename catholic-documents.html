Official Catholic Sources

One Church Documents Library

This library provides organised access to official documents maintained by the Holy See and Vatican.

The original documents remain on official Church websites so that visitors receive the authoritative text rather than an altered or outdated copy.

Document Types Popes Bulls & Briefs Councils Doctrine & Law
About this library: This ministry does not alter official Church documents. External links open documents maintained by the Holy See. Historical digitisation varies, so some older documents may be available only in Latin, Italian, or another language.
