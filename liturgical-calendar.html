The Church Year

The Catholic liturgical year celebrates the mysteries of Christ's life, from His Incarnation and birth to His Passion, Resurrection, and the coming of the Holy Spirit. Through the seasons of the Church, believers are invited to grow in faith and holiness.

Advent Season

Advent is the season of preparation and hope before Christmas. The Church remembers Christ's first coming at Bethlehem and prepares for His second coming in glory.

Meaning

A time of prayer, waiting, repentance, and joyful expectation.

Devotions

Advent wreath, Novena prayers, Scripture meditation, and acts of charity.

Christmas Season

The Christmas season celebrates the mystery of the Incarnation: Jesus Christ, the Son of God, becoming man for our salvation.

Lent and Holy Week

Lent is a forty-day journey of prayer, fasting, and almsgiving that prepares Christians to celebrate the Passion, Death, and Resurrection of Jesus.

Ash Wednesday

The beginning of Lent and a reminder of conversion and repentance.

Holy Week

The Church enters into the mystery of Christ's suffering, death, and victory.

Easter Season

Easter celebrates the Resurrection of Jesus Christ and His victory over sin and death. It continues until Pentecost, when the Holy Spirit descends upon the Apostles.

Ordinary Time

Ordinary Time is a season of growth in which Christians reflect on the teachings, miracles, and ministry of Jesus Christ.

Traditional Catholic Devotions

Month of Mary

May is traditionally dedicated to the Blessed Virgin Mary.

Sacred Heart of Jesus

June is traditionally dedicated to the Sacred Heart of Jesus.

Holy Rosary

October is traditionally dedicated to the Holy Rosary.

Holy Souls

November is a special time of prayer for the faithful departed.
