Lectio Divina

Lectio Divina is the traditional Catholic method of praying with Sacred Scripture.

1. Read (Lectio)

Read the passage slowly and attentively.

2. Meditate (Meditatio)

Reflect on what God is saying through the passage.

3. Pray (Oratio)

Speak to God from your heart in response to His Word.

4. Contemplate (Contemplatio)

Rest quietly in God's presence and allow Him to transform your heart.
