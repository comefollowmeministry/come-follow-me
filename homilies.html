Featured Homily

Theme: Following Christ
Scripture: Matthew 4:18–22
Category: Vocation and Discipleship

Leaving the Nets Behind

When Jesus called Peter and Andrew, He did not merely invite them to admire Him from a distance. He called them to leave their nets and begin a new way of life.

The nets represent more than fishing equipment. They can represent fear, attachment, habit, pride, comfort, and anything that prevents a person from responding freely to God.

Following Christ does not always mean abandoning a profession or travelling to another country. For every Christian, it means allowing Jesus to direct our choices, relationships, responsibilities, and service.

The call of Christ is personal, but it is never private. Jesus forms disciples so that they may become instruments of mercy, truth, reconciliation, and hope for others.

“Come, follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.”

Matthew 4:19

The important question is not only whether Christ is calling. He is always calling. The deeper question is: what must I leave behind so that I can follow Him more faithfully?
