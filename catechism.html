What is the Catechism?

The Catechism of the Catholic Church is the official summary of the faith and teaching of the Catholic Church. It presents what Catholics believe, celebrate, live, and pray, drawing from Sacred Scripture, Sacred Tradition, and the Church's Magisterium.

Read the Official Catechism

Part One: The Profession of Faith

This section explains the Apostles' Creed and the Nicene Creed, revealing God's plan of salvation, the Trinity, creation, the Church, and eternal life.

Part Two: The Celebration of the Christian Mystery

This part teaches about the seven sacraments, the liturgy, the Holy Mass, and the sacramental life of the Church.

Part Three: Life in Christ

Learn about the Ten Commandments, the Beatitudes, Christian morality, virtue, freedom, justice, charity, and living as a disciple of Christ.

Part Four: Christian Prayer

This section explores prayer in the Christian life, the Lord's Prayer, and the Church's rich tradition of personal and communal prayer.
