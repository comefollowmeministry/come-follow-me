Prayer Request Guidelines

  • Do not include passwords, bank details, identification documents, or financial information.
  • Avoid sharing another person’s private medical or family information without permission.
  • Prayer requests are not a substitute for emergency, medical, legal, or professional assistance.
  • For urgent danger or medical emergencies, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
  • Use this page respectfully and only for genuine intentions.
